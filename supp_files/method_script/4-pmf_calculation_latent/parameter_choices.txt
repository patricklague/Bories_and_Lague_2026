Parameter choices in pmf-from-distribution.py (latent-logarithm PMF)
=====================================================================

Sampling and blocks. Each analog was simulated in 3 independent trajectories
(n_trajectories = 3). The 400-1000 ns production window is split into 3
non-overlapping 200 ns blocks (n_batches = 3), giving n = 9 blocks treated as
independent samples. This assumes 200 ns is longer than the correlation time
of the solute depth (assumed, not measured here). mode = "total" uses every solute molecule. The sampling
depth N of a block (number of molecule-frames, 520 000, or 260 000 for the "-1"
analogs) is taken from the raw data (raw_data_summary.dat, otherwise the raw
line count). With it, the stored 6-decimal densities convert back to exact
integer counts. If the rounding residual exceeds 0.45 count, or the total
differs from N by more than 1 %, a warning flags the mismatch.

Physical constants and grid. k_B = 0.008314 kJ mol-1 K-1 and T = 303.15 K are
the simulation temperature, so PMFs are in kJ/mol. z_bin = 1.0 A is the
histogram bin width of the distribution files. The +z and -z bins are summed
onto a |z| grid. The grid is extended to 50 A when the data stop earlier, and
these extra bins count as genuine zero-count observations.

Water reference. recal_lo = 40 A and recal_hi = 50 A define the bulk-water
window: it lies well outside the bilayer, where the PMF is flat. The reference
a is the inverse-variance weighted mean of the untranslated block-averaged PMF
over this window. Bins whose SE is <= 1e-9 kJ/mol (se_floor) are identical
across blocks, so their SE is floating-point noise. If the window contains such
a bin, an unweighted mean is used instead, to avoid arbitrary weights.

Latent logarithm (Biswas 2016). One Normal prior on the log-rates is shared by
all bins of the 9 blocks, as Biswas recommends for linked datasets. Exposures
are expressed as N/K (expected count per bin of a uniform histogram, K bins),
so that latent rates are O(1). This makes the paper's initialisation,
mean/variance of log(t/o + 1), meaningful; the units only affect the starting
point, not the result. The initial variance is floored at 1e-6 to avoid
division by zero. Iterated conditional modes stop when mu and the relative
change of sigma^2 vary by less than lag_tol = 1e-8. lag_max_iter = 5000 is a
safety cap; in practice convergence takes 10-17 iterations, and a different
initialisation gives the same mu and sigma. The per-bin Newton-Raphson MAP
step (strictly concave objective) uses a tolerance of 1e-10, at most 200
iterations, and steps clipped to +/-5 log units, which prevents overflow of
exp(z) far from the optimum.

Bootstrap. n_bootstrap = 10 000 replicates keep the Monte Carlo noise of the
bootstrap SE below 1 % (tested: 0.7 % spread across 20 seeds; 2.4 % at 1 000
and 4.7 % at 200 replicates). This is well above the 50-200 replicates usual
for a standard error (Efron & Tibshirani 1993), and still cheap (~10 s per
analog including the lag refit). bootstrap_seed = 2025 is arbitrary and only
makes the output reproducible; another seed changes the SE by ~0.7 %. Each
replicate resamples the 9 count histograms with replacement and repeats the
whole procedure (lag fit, averaging, reference, shift). small_sample_correction
= True multiplies the bootstrap SE by sqrt(n/(n-1)) = 1.061 for n = 9. This
removes the known downward bias of the bootstrap variance of a mean,
((n-1)/n) s^2/n, so the SE matches the conventional s/sqrt(n) block SE (tested
on sca: 0.240 kJ/mol corrected vs 0.240 analytical vs 0.226 uncorrected).
bootstrap_chunk = 1000 only sets how many replicates are processed at once
(memory/speed trade-off) and does not affect the results.
