
For each analog, the PMF was computed as F(z) = −k_BT ln p(z) from the symmetrized density distribution of each of nine 200‑ns blocks (three blocks per trajectory, three independent trajectories). The nine block PMFs were averaged without any prior alignment. The bulk‑water reference, a, was the inverse‑variance‑weighted mean of the averaged PMF over 40 Å ≤ |z| ≤ 50 Å, and the PMF was shifted so that this reference equals zero. Statistical uncertainties were estimated by a nonparametric block bootstrap (10,000 replicates). In each replicate, the nine blocks were resampled with replacement, and the averaging, reference estimation and shift were repeated. The standard error at each z was the standard deviation of the bootstrap distribution, corrected for small‑sample bias by a factor √(n/(n−1)) (n = 9). This approach propagates the uncertainty of the reference while fully accounting for its correlation with the PMF values. Consequently, the errors are not artificially inflated inside the reference region.

Umbrella Sampling

Umbrella‑sampling PMFs were obtained with WHAM for each of three consecutive 50‑ns blocks. Within each block, the profiles of the two solutes, restrained in opposite leaflets, were folded onto |z| and averaged. The three block PMFs were then averaged without prior alignment. The bulk‑water reference, a, was the inverse‑variance‑weighted mean of the averaged PMF over 30 Å ≤ |z| ≤ 37.8 Å, and the PMF was shifted so that this reference equals zero. Uncertainties were estimated by an exact nonparametric block bootstrap: all 3³ = 27 resamples of the blocks with replacement were enumerated. In each resample, the averaging, reference estimation and shift were repeated, and the standard error was the standard deviation of the resulting profiles, multiplied by √(n/(n−1)) to correct for small‑sample bias. This approach propagates the uncertainty of the reference while accounting for its correlation with the PMF, and it makes the result essentially independent of the arbitrary additive constant of each WHAM profile.
